Responsible Growth: Implementing the Sustainability Report

The publication of our Sustainability Report reinforces KOS Group’s commitment to disclosing non-financial information with greater transparency towards all stakeholders.

The need to adopt a sustainable and resilient business model — along with the will to improve social, economic and environmental sustainability — has led to the creation of our first Sustainability Plan.

This programme outlines clear objectives through to 2025, positioning KOS as a key driver of healthcare system sustainability, while remaining true to its mission: improving patients’ quality of life.

Sustainability Report

Sustainability Plan

KOS Group’s Sustainability Plan is structured around three main focus areas, which reflect our priority commitments for 2025. Each area includes specific sustainability challenges, monitored through KPIs and targets.

People and Communities 2024

We are committed to developing increasingly responsible service delivery models that create a positive impact on both individuals and the wider communities we serve.

Supporting professional growth:

  • Increase the number of annual training hours for employees and external staff
  • Promote a people-centred approach
  • Encourage onboarding completion for new hires through corporate culture training

Ensuring a safe and motivating work environment:

  • Reduce the gender pay gap
  • Improve employee satisfaction and engagement
  • Lower the incidence of workplace injuries

Contributing to local community impact:

  • Increase the number of local sustainability projects
Quality of Care 2024

We are committed to delivering high-quality services that continuously improve the lives of patients and clients.

Driving continuous service improvement:

  • Improve the Net Promoter Score (NPS)
  • Improve results from internal clinical audits
  • Expand the use of Electronic Health Records across facilities

Ensuring compliance and ethical practice:

  • Improve performance in internal administrative and management audits
  • Screen suppliers based on ESG certifications as part of the procurement evaluation process
Energy Efficiency 2024

We aim to continuously enhance the quality of services provided, while ensuring transparency, regulatory compliance and accessibility of information.

Reducing emissions and optimising energy use:

  • Increase the share of electricity from renewable sources
  • Reduce overall energy consumption

Minimising waste and promoting recycling and reuse of resources:

  • Reduce food waste
